Texture Guide

Bundlish textures are selected to help customers match their natural hair pattern and styling preference.

4b–4c

Coil

Made from 100% premium hair with tight zigzag coils that blend with Type 4b–4c hair. Great for wash-n-go’s, twist-outs, braid-outs, and rod sets.

3c–4a

Ria

The in-between texture of Coil and Curl, crafted with soft springy curls. Ideal for versatile, everyday natural styles.

3a–3b

Curl

Loose s-pattern curls for a soft, elongated curly look that is easy to manage and blends with Type 3a–3b hair.

Blow-out texture

Natural Straight

An airy, voluminous finish that blends with blown-out or lightly straightened natural hair.

FAQs

Quick answers before you order.

How do I place an order?

Order through Instagram DM or WhatsApp. WhatsApp: 026 497 7719. Instagram: @bundlish.

How many bundles do I need for a sew-in?

We recommend 2–3 wefted hair bundles for a full head in all textures. For lengths 20 inches and above, at least 3 bundles are recommended.

How long does shipping take for Ghana?

Available orders are processed same day and delivered next business day. Pre-orders take 1–2 weeks to process, then delivery is next business day once available.

How long does international shipping take?

Available orders are processed within 2–3 working days. Transit is usually 3–7 working days through DHL, FedEx, or UPS. Pre-orders take 1–2 weeks to process.

What products should I use?

Use Bundlish shampoo and conditioner bar, a gentle co-wash, or sulfate-free shampoo. Follow with rinse-out conditioner. Avoid heavy creams and butters.

How many sets of clip-ins do I need?

One set is recommended for a full head.

What colour do the extensions come in?

The hair is unprocessed and natural in colour, ranging from dark brown to soft natural black. For colour customisation, email info@bundlish.com or send a DM.

How long will the hair last?

With proper care, Bundlish extensions can last up to 24 months or more. Bleaching and excessive heat can reduce the lifespan.
